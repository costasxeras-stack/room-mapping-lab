# 0002: Treat the threshold as a business decision

**Status:** accepted, 0.1.0

## Context

Teams often set one confidence threshold on model metrics alone.

## Decision

Model net value from volume, review capacity, review cost, booking share, cost of a wrong room and value of a mapped name, and show where the best threshold sits and how it moves.

## Tradeoff

- The inputs are estimates until finance and operations agree them
- The discussion moves from model accuracy to money and risk
